# Your first day in Season One

This walk-through takes a new player from the landing page to a first invite. It takes about ten minutes. Nothing in the game costs money.

## 1. Look around before you sign in

Open the game. The landing page shows:

- the season clock, counting down to the end of the season;
- the leaderboard of the points race;
- live drops: rare, epic and legendary finds of other players as they happen;
- the prize fund and the public treasury that holds it.

Links at the bottom lead to the season rules, the odds and "How we can't rig it".

## 2. Sign in

1. Press "Start playing".
2. Tick "I am 18 or older and accept the season rules". The sign-in buttons stay off until you tick it.
3. Press "Connect wallet" and sign the message in your wallet. Signing is free: no transaction, no gas.
4. Pick a name: 3 to 20 characters, letters, digits, dot, dash or underscore. Other players see it in the tables and the live feed.

"Continue with email" is not open yet. It opens before the season starts.

## 3. Meet your hero

You land on the Hero tab. Your hero fights one wave every minute, on its own.

- Each wave gives 10 points plus your gear power.
- Every 10th wave has a chance of a find: an item for one of four slots (weapon, armor, charm, boots).
- A better item goes on by itself. Gear power adds up: common 1, uncommon 2, rare 3, epic 4, legendary 5.
- After you leave, the hero keeps fighting for 8 hours. Come back at least once a day to keep it going.

When you return, the top panel tells you what happened while you were away: waves, points and finds.

## 4. Open your daily chest

1. On the Hero tab, press "Open daily chest".
2. Watch the reel stop on your item. A receipt follows with the server's seal and your own number.
3. Press "Back to hero". If the item is better than what you wear, it is already on.

One daily chest a day, reset at 00:00 UTC. Open it on several days in a row and your streak grows, up to 7. Each streak step raises the chance of rare, epic and legendary. A missed day costs one step.

The bar under the chest counts days without a rare or better item. On the third such day a rare chest waits for you on the Chests tab. It always holds rare or better.

## 5. Check that the chest was fair

A roll stays sealed until the next UTC day. Tomorrow:

1. Open "How we can't rig it" from the Hero tab.
2. Press "Check yesterday".
3. Your browser checks every roll of yesterday and shows "Verified".

The check runs in your browser, not on our server. Why this works: [Fairness and season design](explanation-fairness-and-season.md).

## 6. Invite a friend

1. Open the Friends tab.
2. Press "Copy invite link" and send it to a friend.
3. Your friend signs in through the link and shows up in "Your friends" with "0 / 3 days".

A friend counts after playing on 3 different days. Each counted friend brings you 10% of their wave points, up to 50,000 points a season. Counted friends also put you in the invite race, which holds 30% of the prize fund.

You can also share a rare or better find: open its receipt and press "Share on X" or "Share on Telegram". The link shows a picture of your find and carries your invite.

## 7. Watch the race

The Season tab shows both races: points and invites. A line marks the last prize place. Your own row shows how far you are from that line.

Play on 3 different days during Season One and you get the Founder badge. It shows next to your name in the tables, the live feed and on share cards.
